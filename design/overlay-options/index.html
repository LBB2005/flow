Flow overlay: five floating pop-ups

The dictation pop-up leaves the notch and floats above the Dock. Each preview runs the same dictation in sync: listen, think, paste into the chat box, dismiss. Open one full screen to step through every state, switch the backdrop or drive the waveform with your own mic.

Backdrop
Hold every preview on

On a full-screen page: 1 to 7 jump to a state, Space plays the whole flow, H dismisses, T switches the backdrop, M uses your mic, and Esc while listening cancels, as it does in the app.

Copy and timings come from the app: messages from PillState, 600 ms hold after pasting, 2 s for the other outcomes, bars sampled every 120 ms with 0.55 smoothing, loudness mapped from −50 to 0 dBFS.
